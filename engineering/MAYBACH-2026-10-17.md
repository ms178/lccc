## Red-team findings and changes

1. Merely forwarding no_builtins into sema is insufficient. The post-inline
   strlen pass independently folded ordinary names. It now receives the mode.
2. Math simplification and external purity inference were independent bypasses:
   sqrt could become an instruction and unused strlen could disappear under
   -fno-builtin. Math folding now uses a scoped thread-local policy restored
   even on early returns; purity inference receives the TU policy explicitly.
   Source-declared pure/const and inferred user-body properties remain valid.
3. Unknown -f flags must fail regardless of -v order. The fallback now has
   explicit no-op/refused entries, including LTO/ASan and other security families.
   This is NOT a complete generated registry: all existing stateful arms and
   unknown -mno/-W paths still need the MB-01 inventory follow-up.
4. Freestanding mode changes __STDC_HOSTED__ and ordinary aliases; explicit
   compiler builtin interfaces remain. Partial builtin/vectorizer controls are
   refused rather than pretending independent support. Full tree-vectorize
   disable gates map, SLP, memory/SSA ARX and interleaving entries.
5. Static strlen literal folding also appears to count beyond an embedded NUL.
   Static initializer default-mode lowering previously panicked in the earlier
   session. These need permanent witnesses; neither is closed by the VLA fix.
