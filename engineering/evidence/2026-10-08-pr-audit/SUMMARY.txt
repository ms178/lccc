3. THE COST PR #777 REPORTS IS REAL, AND IS NOT NECESSARY
   Callgrind Ir, PR #777 vs main:  histogram 1.08034 (+8.03%), glibc_strstr
   1.04097 (+4.10%) -- both reproduce its claimed numbers exactly.
   Root cause of the histogram cost, traced: a constant trip count lets the
   unroller run, the unroller spells the latch as a CHAIN of unit adds
   (v60=v19+1, v61=v60+1, v62=v61+1, v63=v62+1), iv_widen's recognisers require
   the latch to name the phi directly, so the counted loop is never widened, the
   index stays U32, and the SIB displacement fold is (correctly) unsound.
   Three fixes in this tree, each with its own proof and tests:
     (a) canonicalise the final chain link to `phi + total` -- value-identical by
         associativity of modular addition, no bound argument, zero cost;
     (b) generalise theorem 5 to mag>1 with a constant bound (n <= max-mag+1);
     (c) tighten the seed range to n-1+mag, without which the unrolled body's
         i+1/i+2/i+3 members leave the narrow type and the closure aborts.
   Plus a pre-existing defect neither PR touches: the SIB cast peel used the
   VALUE-only predicate, so a pointer-width index (`size_t i`, the shape gzip,
   zlib-ng, expat, SQLite and glibc strings are written in) never reached the
   affine fold.  generation.rs now uses the pointer-ring arm on LP64.

4b. PERF-9, MEASURED AND DELIBERATELY HELD BACK
   The SIB cast peel tests the VALUE-only predicate, so a pointer-width index
   (`size_t i`) never reaches the affine displacement fold.  Enabling the tree's
   own cast_preserves_offset_value arm on LP64 is sound and locally profitable:
   histogram Ir 1.06025 -> 0.95983, w_s8 goes from `leaq 1(%rsi),%rsi` +
   `movq (%rdi,%rsi,8)` to `movq 8(%rdi,%rsi,8)`.  It is NOT in this tree, for
   two measured reasons:
     (i)  it breaks contract 2 of tests/regression/check_select_from_compare.sh.
          In ascii_case_fold's byte-fold kernel the fold frees %r13/%r14/%r15 and
          the fourth lane then loses its cmovbe fusion, materialising
          `setbe %r15b` + movzbl + movsbq + `testl` (+1 insn, Ir 1.00740).  main
          PASSES that gate; the tree with the arm FAILS it.  No knob reproduces
          the loss (CCC_NO_IV_WIDEN, CCC_NO_GEP_FOLD, CCC_NO_PF06_ADD_PEEL,
          CCC_NO_FLAG_PEEPHOLES all leave setbe=1), no data directive sits in the
          fusion window, and -march makes no difference (identical with and
          without x86-64-v3), so PR #725's classifier and the march are both
          excluded: it is a fold/RA interaction.
     (ii) it interferes with the widening's own wins: the 402 matrix moves
          98002 -> 97984 (-18) with the arm, but 98002 -> 97931 (-71) without it,
          because i686_alu_chains (-12 x3), struct_copy (-6), pixmap (-5 x2) and
          csv_field_sum (-4 x2, -2) only improve once it stops competing for
          registers.
   On ILP32 the arm is sound but unprofitable outright (+60 insns vs -18 on
   x86-64).  Recorded in-tree at the peel site so the next reader inherits the
   numbers instead of the investigation.
