## Landed (this session)

1. **Acc dest popcnt/lzcnt/tzcnt without staging movq.**
   `emit_bitcount_direct` writes `popcntl %edi, %eax` instead of
   `movq %rdi, %rax; popcntl %eax, %eax`.
2. **Register-direct Clz/Ctz** (same two-operand form as Popcount).
3. **Intel false-dependency xor** before popcnt/lzcnt/tzcnt when dest ≠ src
   (`xorl %eax, %eax; popcntl %edi, %eax` — GCC's shape).
4. **PF-16 `movl` on `operand_to_rax`** for non-negative imm32.
5. **PF-16 `movl` on `operand_to_rcx`**.
6. **`const_as_imm32_typed` in `emit_alu_reg_direct`** — Knuth hash
   `imull $-1640531535, %edi, %edi` instead of `movabsq` + `imull %eax`.
7. **MachInst S32 unsigned imm32** (`const_as_imm32_size` / `emit_alu_operand_r`).
8. **MachInst `cmp $0` → `test`** (`lower_cmp` and `lower_cmp_branch`).
9. **32-bit unsigned `cmov`** (`emit_cmov_typed`; signed I32 stays `cmovq`
   because `cmovl` zero-extends dest).
10. **`is_read_modify_write` dest-only** for popcnt/lzcnt/tzcnt/andn/blsr/blsi/bzhi.
11. **`is_full_write` dest-only** for the same mnemonics (syntactic DCE fallback).
12. **`is_32bit_zero_extend_write` += popcntl/lzcntl/tzcntl/andnl/blsrl/blsil/bzhil**
    (kills identity `movl %eax, %eax` / `cltq` after bitcounts).
13. **Skip identity `movl %eax, %eax`** in `operand_to_rax_i32`.
14. **Acc-path `imull $imm` typed** (`try_emit_acc_immediate`).
15. **`candidate_uses_32bit_dest` += popcntl/lzcntl/tzcntl/andnl**
    (movq→movl copy narrowing after a 32-bit bitcount).
