## Godbolt oracles (GCC 16.2, Clang 23.1, ICC 2021.10, ICX latest)

Kernels: `engineering/kernels/popcount_swar.c`,
`engineering/kernels/loop_rotate_sum.c`.

### popcount32, `-O3 -march=x86-64` (baseline, no POPCNT)

| compiler | insns | shape |
|---|---|---|
| LCCC | 20 | SWAR + push/pop %rcx + redundant `movl %eax,%eax` |
| Clang 23.1 | 16 | same SWAR, no save/restore, reuses `%edi` |
| ICC 2021.10 | 16 | same SWAR |
| ICX latest | 16 | same SWAR |
| GCC 16.2 | 5 | **libgcc call** `__popcountdi2` (not inlined) |

LCCC's inline SWAR is the right baseline (no libgcc). Extra cost vs
Clang is the conservative %rcx save (accumulator-mode may have staged
a live value there — do **not** drop blindly) and a redundant zext.

### popcount32, `-O3 -march=x86-64-v3` (hardware POPCNT)

| compiler | insns | asm |
|---|---|---|
| Clang 23.1 / ICX | 2 | `popcntl %edi, %eax; ret` |
| GCC 16.2 | 3 | `xorl %eax,%eax; popcntl %edi,%eax; ret` (false-dep break) |
| LCCC | 4 | `movq %rdi,%rax; popcntl %edi,%eax; movl %eax,%eax; ret` |
| ICC 2021.10 | 16 | still SWAR (`-march=x86-64-v3` not honoured on CE's icc) |

Dead `movq %rdi,%rax` before `popcntl %edi,%eax` is leftover staging.
Follow-up, not a miscompile.

### sum_arr, `-O2`, LCCC with `CCC_LOOP_ROTATE=1` + vectorize off

GCC 16.2: 12-insn pointer-IV do-while. Clang 23.1 vectorizes at -O2
