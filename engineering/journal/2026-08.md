## 08-28 — kernel-boot milestone + deferred `.skip` two-bug diagnosis
<!-- src: engineering/FOLLOWUP-2026-08-28-kernel-boot.md, scripts/FOLLOWUP_BOOT_GATE.md -->

First full kernel build with `CC=lccc LD=lccc-ld`: 1015 translation units,
0 frontend errors; 32 KiB setup gate **PASS** (`_end = 0x7600` of
`0x8000`; the ARM/RISC-V peephole wave S10–S12b in the same week). The
hybrid bisection result that stood for weeks:

| setup (`arch/x86/boot`) | payload (`vmlinux`) | result |
|---|---|---|
| lccc | gcc 14.2 | **BOOTS** |
| gcc 14.2 | lccc | HANGS (silent) |

⇒ the real-mode/setup stage is exonerated; the defect is in the vmlinux
payload (continued at 09-07 C6 in [`2026-09-W2.md`](2026-09-W2.md)).

Deferred-`.skip` machinery: **two** distinct bugs, both found — (A)
speculative jump-shortening ran before section sizing, freezing long
jumps with pre-reduction sizes; (B) `altinstr` fill wrote padding at the
pre-shrink offset. Fix: byte-fill runtime check added (Case 2c). The
`scripts/FOLLOWUP_BOOT_GATE.md` record (renamed from the 09-02 note)
also carried the first i686 dead-frame-elision peephole and the setup
`.text` oracle: GCC `.text` 10154/`_end` 0x4200 vs lccc 23620/0x898c —
the boot-size gap is RA quality, not a single pattern. A **negative
control** (`gcc`-everything boot) was never recorded for the failure
cell — noted as method debt.

## 08-29 — early-IDT `.fill` over-pad
<!-- src: engineering/FOLLOWUP-2026-08-29-early-idt-fill.md -->

64-bit kernel reached long mode, halted in `early_fixup_exception`
(`pv_ops native_halt`); `pt_regs.ip = ffffffff83903aa7` (the `endbr64`
of the #GP stub). Root: the assembler's `.fill` padded
`early_idt_handler_array` stubs *past* `EARLY_IDT_HANDLER_SIZE = 13 with
IBT`, so `IDT[n]` pointed mid-stub. Not-a-bug record (do not
re-investigate): CPUID/FPU userspace. [fix]

## 08-29 — `-u` on script-driven links
<!-- src: engineering/FOLLOWUP-2026-08-29-ld-script-undefined.md -->

`arch/x86/boot/header.o` failed with "32-bit and 64-bit EFI entry points
do not match": `zoffset.h` had no `ZO_efi32_stub_entry` because the
linker dropped `-u`-forced symbols from an archive member pulled in by
the link script. Fix: script-driven links must force-resolve
`--undefined` members *before* section collection. [fix]

## 08-29 → 08-30 — matmul trilogy (96 → 74 → 72 insns)
<!-- src: engineering/FOLLOWUP-2026-08-29-matmul-broadcast-hoist.md, FOLLOWUP-2026-08-29-matmul-iv-i64.md, FOLLOWUP-2026-08-29-matmul-sib-hoisted.md -->

`tests/benchmark/programs/matmul.c` at `-O3 -march=x86-64-v3`, GCC 16.2
= 25 insns, Clang 23.1 = 438:

1. **Broadcast hoist** (`transform_to_fma_f64x4` invariant path):
   moved the per-iteration `vmovsd (%r15) + vbroadcastsd` out of the
   inner loop (the `A[i][k]` scalar is loop-invariant). Inner loop
   gained the hoisted `FmaF64x4Hoisted` intrinsic. LBB6: 4 broadcasts
   → 0. [goal]
2. **IV promotion to I64**: the i32 byte-offset IV forced two
   `movslq`/iter. Promoting the vector byte IV retired one (the
   `addl` zero-extends; the other is the live-out). [goal]
3. **SIB + folded disp**: the 3-chunk unroll re-created 6 Adds + 6
   GEPs that the backend lowered as 11 `leaq` + 8 `movq`. Red-team
   found the hoist re-introducing address arithmetic → fold GEP
   offsets into SIB displacements with hoisted bases. [goal]

Method note kept verbatim: *audit the fix after it lands — the previous
step re-created the problem one level down*.

## 08-31 — SCCP adoption audit: seven upstream defects
<!-- src: engineering/FOLLOWUP-2026-08-31-sccp-loop-rotate-ir-verifier.md (SCCP half) -->

Adapted Wegman–Zadeck SCCP + CSR use-def from
`CrazyTodd-one/claudes-c-compiler` — **not** adopted as-is; its IR
assumptions miscompile on ours. Defects fixed (see
[`../DECISIONS.md`](../DECISIONS.md) 2026-08-31 fork-survey for the
adopt/reject table): `asm goto` edges invisible to the solver
(critical; `cfg_simplify` honours them, the lattice didn't); folded
branches not pruning dead-edge phi entries; an open `default` arm
fabricating constants (`⊤ ⊓ C = C` — replaced with closed catch-all:
any unmodelled dest-producing op lowers to `Overdefined`); `is_nonzero`
disagreeing with `cfg_simplify` on `long double`; x87-80 folding
through `f64` (divergent oracle); `IsConstant` resolved negatively too
early (phase-dependent). Kill switches: `CCC_SCCP_NO_{PRUNE,DEFS,SUBST,FOLD}`
/ `CCC_SCCP_TRACE_PRUNE`; whole-pass `CCC_DISABLE_PASSES=sccp`. Runs in
main phase 4 (before `constant_fold`) and in `run_inline_phase` before
the cleanup `cfg_simplify`; `NUM_PASSES` stays 11. [fix][infra]

## 08-31 — `loop_rotate` stale-phi + the IR verifier is born
<!-- src: engineering/FOLLOWUP-2026-08-31-sccp-loop-rotate-ir-verifier.md (verifier half) -->

Two-block carried-phi bug in `loop_unroll` (header phi got
`Copy(final)` instead of `Copy(init)` for outside uses; upstream PF-17
fixed the same bug independently with opposite polarity and theirs was
correct — recorded so it isn't re-litigated). More importantly, the
session wrote the **structural IR verifier**: 396/1112 violations
found at baseline, driven to 0 across all six opt levels (3354
configs), wired as permanent gate (`CCC_VERIFY_IR=1|abort|strict`).
Loop-rotate default-enable was measured **negative** (0.87× adler32) —
do-not-retry recorded in DECISIONS. The namechars classifier attempt
was reverted the same day: needs critical-edge splitting of the shared
increment block then SESE if-conversion (worst kernel 0.44×); backlog
PF-CLS-1 owns the retry. [fix][infra][neg]

## 08-31 — sqlite SIB call-clobber (multi-def stretched required span)
<!-- src: engineering/FOLLOWUP-2026-08-31-sqlite-sib-call-clobber.md -->

sqlite 3.53.4 `CREATE TABLE t(a);` SIGSEGV in `sqlite3DbStrNDup` <-
`vdbeChangeP4Full`: `%r10` (the I32 Copy dest of `n` after the `n==0`
diamond) was read by `movb $0,(%rbp,%r10,1)` after lookaside+memcpy
clobbered it. Root: the liveness required-span for a **multi-def**
value was stretched past the call; fix tightens the SIB liveness
window. [fix]
