## 09-15 — `.gnu.hash` parity on five backends + RELRO + riscv DT_NEEDED
<!-- src: FOLLOWUP-2026-09-15-gnuhash-relro-crossarch.md -->

Measured oracle truth (20k absent probes): lld nbuckets = n/4, bloom =
next_pow2(n/4), shift 26 (FPR ≈0.4–0.6 %); mold ≈ n/8; bfd wants a
per-link records and 1–2 %; **old lccc: one bloom word, shift 6 — the
bloom filter died above ~30 exports (~1.0 false-positive at 20k)**.
Land rule (helper `linker_common::hash.rs`): `bloom_words = max(1,
next_pow2(ceil(n/4)))` — power of two required, glibc masks the word
index with `size-1`; `shift = lg2(bloom_words × class_bits)`;
`nbuckets = max(1, n/4)`. At n=3000 lccc-built `.so` is byte-identical
to lld's geometry with fewer total bytes than bfd/lld/mold. Bloom
carries the false-positive rate (lld's 8-bits-per-symbol budget);
buckets only bound chain length — bfd's 2× buckets are strictly worse.
Same pass: RELRO page-rightsizing fixed on riscv, landed on aarch64;
riscv `-l` `DT_NEEDED` generation; hidden defects found while doing it
(two, fixed in the same series). Validation: 239/0/0 linker suite. [goal][fix]

## 09-16 — BB-SLP v5: packed lane shifts / rotates / minmax
<!-- src: engineering/FOLLOWUP-2026-09-16-bb-slp-v5-shifts-rotates-minmax.md -->

16 new intrinsics: `VecShl/VecLShr/VecAShr` × {I16x8, I16x16, I32x4,
I32x8, I64x2, I64x4} (LShr only for I64) — **no packed byte shift and
no packed qword AShr exist before AVX-512, so those (type, op) pairs
have no intrinsic and the pack builder rejects the lanes** (the ABI of
the family: emitters assert the per-family amount bound 15/31/63).
Packed rotate/minmax families follow the S06–S09 discipline (vecreg
homing, deferred-dest-store survival, 128-bit VEX memfold); one
instruction per homed shape under VEX.128, SSE2-only shapes stage through
the destination home or `%xmm0`. [goal]

## 09-16 — the loop epics: adler32 DO8 + nbody (design record)
<!-- src: FOLLOWUP-2026-09-16-slp-v6-loop-epics.md -->

Design for the reassociation-needing epics explicitly outside BB-SLP's
charter. Recent ground truth: lccc's adler32 DO8 is a clean scalar
chain (8× `movzbl + lea/add`, 3 insns/byte, 16-add loop-carried
dependency) — **GCC 14.2 -O2 emits the same scalar shape**; only zlib's
hand-written `adler32_ssse3` is packed. The opportunity is the
matrix/chain reassociation GCC/ICX perform for the nbody-class kernel;
the soundness proof sketch, lowering shapes and test contracts are the
durable payload — kept as the spec for PF finds in `backlog.md`. [rec]
